# Character Bible — Campus Life: The Journey

The initial cast for the main story. Everyone here is **fictional**; any
resemblance to real people is unintended. UCIS University (*Until Christ is
Seen*) is fictional too.

Companion documents: [GAME_DESIGN.md](GAME_DESIGN.md),
[STORYLINE.md](STORYLINE.md).

**Status:** design only. No character in this document exists in the game yet;
Phase 1 has a single procedural placeholder avatar.

---

## 1. Writing rules for this cast

- **Nobody is simply good or evil.** Every character has a real virtue, a real
  flaw and a reason for what they do.
- **Respect the university's faith identity.** UCIS is church-founded. Faith
  is part of campus life (chapel, fellowship, grace before meals) and is never
  the butt of a joke. Wrongdoing belongs to individuals who fall short of the
  university's ideals, never to the faith itself.
- **No stereotypes as shortcuts.** Ethnicity, region, religion, class and
  gender never decide a character's role, intelligence or morality. The
  northern student is the top engineer; the wealthy student is not the villain;
  the administrator believes he is helping.
- **Grounded in contemporary Ghanaian campus life:** levels (100–400), hostels,
  group chats, mobile money, SRC elections, hall week, Friday wear, *dumsor*,
  printing queues, past questions.
- **Language:** natural Ghanaian English. Twi, Ga, Ewe, Dagbani, Fante or Pidgin
  phrases appear where a person would really use them, and are understandable
  in context.
- **Relationship values** start at the *initial attitude* shown for each
  character, on the −100 to +100 scale in GAME_DESIGN.md §8.

**Development:** cleared, or loses her scholarship, depending on whether the
player and her friends stand up for her.

**Development:** the player's strongest ally if trusted, a bitter enemy if
publicly accused.

## 4. Character-art direction

The aim is **stylised realism** that sits beside the campus in `GAME_ART/`:
believable proportions and clothing, simplified surfaces, clean shapes,
readable at phone size.

**Concept art is not a game asset.** A drawing or render of a character is a
reference. The game needs a **rigged, skinned, animated 3D model** made from
it. Nothing in this repository is such a model yet.

### Look

- **Proportions:** realistic adult proportions, about 7–7.5 heads tall;
  slightly enlarged hands and heads for readability on small screens.
- **Faces:** simplified, expressive, clearly individual. No uncanny
  photorealism.
- **Skin:** a wide range of deep to medium-brown tones, rendered with warm
  subsurface-style shading and no greyish or ashy shadows. Test every tone under
  the game's afternoon light.
- **Hair:** modelled as shaped cards or sculpted shells for braids, locs,
  twists, cornrows, fades and headwraps. Never generic "default" hair.
- **Clothing:** contemporary Ghanaian student fashion — denim, polos,
  jerseys, sneakers, slides — plus **Friday wear** (print shirts, kaba and
  slit, smocks, kente accents). Patterns are original, not copied from
  registered kente designs.
- **Colour identity:** each story character has one signature accent colour
  so they read at a distance — Nii orange, Abiba indigo, KB white-and-gold,
  Selasi emerald, Esi denim blue, Yaw team red, Hajia Zainab magenta, Dr. Sarpong
  slate, Mr. Narh charcoal.

### Technical specification

| | Hero (story) | Named NPC | Crowd |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Triangles | ≤ 12,000 | ≤ 6,000 | ≤ 3,000 |
| Textures | one 1024² atlas | 512² | 512², shared palettes |
| Materials | ≤ 2 | 1 | 1 |
| Facial animation | blink + mouth open/close | blink | none |
| Format | `.glb` (glTF 2.0) | `.glb` | `.glb` |

- **One shared humanoid skeleton** (about 45 bones, standard humanoid bone
  names) for every character, so all animations are reusable across the cast.
- **Scale and orientation:** metres, +Y up, facing −Z, origin between the feet —
  the same convention as `public/assets/README.md`.
- **Customisation:** the player model is assembled from swappable parts (head,
  hair, top, bottom, shoes) on the shared skeleton, recoloured with material
  parameters rather than extra textures.

### Animation set (minimum)

Idle (×2 variations), walk, run, turn, sit (bench), sit (desk), stand from sit,
talk gestures (×3), phone check, wave, laugh, arms-folded, disappointed,
celebrate. Cinematics reuse these with camera work rather than requiring bespoke
motion capture.

### Deliverables per character

1. Turnaround concept sheet (front, side, back) with colour callouts.
2. Expression sheet (neutral, happy, angry, worried, shocked).
3. Rigged `.glb` on the shared skeleton, within the budget above.
4. In-engine check: under campus lighting, at phone resolution, in both camera
   modes.
